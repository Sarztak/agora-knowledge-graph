DOC_ID: 790
Require each agency that uses, funds, or oversees algorithms to establish an office of civil rights focused on bias, discrimination, and other algorithmic harms.

Mandate these civil rights offices to employ experts and technologists to address issues related to covered algorithms.

Obligate each office of civil rights to submit biennial reports to congressional committees detailing the state of algorithms, risks of bias and discrimination, mitigation steps taken, stakeholder engagement, and recommendations for further actions.

Establish an interagency working group on algorithms and civil rights, led by the Assistant Attorney General of the Civil Rights Division, with membership from each agency's civil rights office.

Authorize necessary appropriations for agencies to implement the Act's provisions.

DOC_ID: 791
Amends title 44 of the United States Code to define "artificial intelligence" and "data governance."

Requires Chief Data Officers to focus on data management and governance, including AI-related data standards.

Mandates the Chief Data Officer Council to enhance data governance, evaluate new technologies, and ensure AI use improves government operations.

Directs the Council to report on data governance progress and provide recommendations for AI and emerging technology adoption, including data transparency and privacy.

Obliges the Director to issue guidance on implementing Council recommendations and maintain a public inventory of Chief Data Officers.

Sets reporting requirements for the Comptroller General on the Council's effectiveness and challenges.

Establishes a sunset clause, rendering the section ineffective after December 14, 2031.

Prohibits the allocation of additional funds to implement the Act.

DOC_ID: 792
Amends the State Department Basic Authorities Act to establish a Deputy Secretary of State for Economic Security, responsible for U.S. economic security matters including sanctions, trade, and supply chains.

Transfers assets, personnel, and duties from existing offices to this new Deputy Secretary role.

Redesignates and relocates various offices and positions, including creating an Assistant Secretary for Technology and Innovation Security, responsible for policy on emerging technologies and research security.

Establishes an Office of Economic Analysis and Targeting to conduct economic analysis, prepare sanctions targeting packages, and manage a federal database on economic security data.

Authorizes $5 million annually for artificial intelligence and machine learning tools to support data analysis.

Allows hiring of additional personnel without standard competitive service requirements to support these new roles and offices.

Designates Foreign Service officers to represent the Deputy Secretary's mission at strategic posts worldwide.

DOC_ID: 793
Amends the Public Health Service Act to establish a comprehensive research program on diagnostic safety and quality, focusing on reducing diagnostic errors and integrating AI tools.

Directs funding for new and existing research centers and mandates stakeholder engagement to enhance diagnostic quality and safety.

Requires the convening of an expert panel, including the Chief Artificial Intelligence Officer, to recommend data types and sources for research improvement.

Establishes an Interagency Council on Improving Diagnosis in Health Care, including members from AI-related departments, to enhance diagnosis quality and identify research gaps.

Mandates the creation of a strategic federal plan to improve diagnosis, with updates and progress reports submitted biennially to Congress.

Authorizes appropriations for these initiatives from 2025 to 2029, ensuring sustained financial support for improving diagnostic practices and integrating AI solutions.

DOC_ID: 794
Directs the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) to establish a consortium representing federal, state, and academic stakeholders. The consortium will use supercomputing and machine learning to develop models that predict adverse health effects from toxic substances, establishing rapid approaches for large-scale identification of toxic substances and the development of safer alternatives.

Requires the Consortium to recommend how the information produced can be used in risk assessments by the EPA, other federal agencies, and industry.

Appropriates funding to the EPA Administrator to carry out the Consortium and requires the Consortium to make its model predictions public and accessible.

DOC_ID: 795
Prohibits the use of federal funds to operate autonomous weapons systems which launch nuclear weapons, or select or engage targets for nuclear weapons, without meaningful human control.

Defines 'meaningful human control' as the ability for humans to control the selection and engagement of targets, as well as the time, location and manner of the use of nuclear weapons.

DOC_ID: 796
Establishes a steering committee to oversee the NAIRR.

Authorizes the National Science Foundation, in coordination with the NAIRR steering committee, to establish the National AI Research Resource.

Creates a NAIRR Program Management Office to manage the NAIRR's day-to-day operations.

Directs the Program Management Office to solicit bids for an Operating Entity for the NAIRR and to oversee that Entity.

Provides for the NAIRR to be resourced with computational resources, data, educational tools and services, and AI testbeds.

Specifies eligibility for access to resources in the NAIRR, including academic, nonprofit, government, and small businesses, among others.

Provides for procedures to establish privacy, ethical, safety and scientific integrity requirements for users of the NAIRR.

Authorizes the NAIRR to charge fees for users at various tiers, including free and subsidized tiers.

Provides for security requirements to prevent unauthorized access and protect research security.

DOC_ID: 797
Provides eight aspirational principles for the responsible global development of artificial intelligence: harmony and friendliness, fairness and justice, inclusivity and sharing, respect of privacy, safety and controllability, shared responsibility, open collaboration, and agile governance.

DOC_ID: 798
Require the state and its entities to conduct Automated Decision System (ADS) Impact Assessments before using any ADS.

Mandate the engagement of a neutral third party to evaluate the ADS, focusing on statistical impacts on protected classes, privacy, and security.

Prohibit purchasing or using ADS without publishing the assessment and considering public comments.

Obligate the state to maintain a list of qualified neutral third parties for conducting assessments.

Require a comprehensive study on the impacts of ADS, particularly on protected classes, to be published within two years.

Demand plain-language notifications to individuals affected by ADS decisions and provide mechanisms for human review.

Ban payments for ADS that do not use open-source software or comply with assessment and policy requirements.

DOC_ID: 799
Prohibits the sale of automated decision tools in New Jersey unless they have been subject to a bias audit in the past year, include a free annual bias audit service that provides results to the purchaser, and discloses that the tool is subject to this bill.

Requires anyone who uses an automated employment decision tool to screen candidates to notify each candidate of using the tool within 30 days.

Provides for collecting civil penalties (fines) if the provisions above are violated.
