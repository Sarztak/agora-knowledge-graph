DOC_ID: 472
Amends the Department of Innovation and Technology Act by establishing the Generative AI and Natural Language Processing Task Force.

Mandates the Task Force to investigate and report on generative AI and natural language processing software.

Comprises the Task Force of government officials, educators, cybersecurity experts, AI experts, business and labor association members, and other stakeholders.

Requires the Task Force to hold at least five public meetings across various Illinois regions in a hybrid format.

Obligates the Task Force to recommend legislation to protect consumer information, suggest model policies for AI use in schools, and assess AI's impact on public services, civil rights, the workforce, and cybersecurity.

Instructs the Department to provide administrative and technical support to the Task Force.

Requires the Task Force to file a report by December 31, 2024, with the Governor and the General Assembly summarizing its findings and recommendations.

DOC_ID: 473
Establishes the "New Jersey Advanced Autonomous Vehicle Task Force" to study and recommend policies for the safe integration of autonomous vehicles on state roads.

Comprises 11 members, including key state officials and public experts appointed by the Governor.

Mandates the task force to hold its initial meeting within 90 days of the law's effective date and to elect a chair from among its members.

Requires the task force to issue a report within 180 days after its first meeting, evaluating existing laws, proposing pilot programs, and suggesting legislative measures.

Entitles the task force to utilize the services of state employees and receive assistance from the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission, Department of Transportation, and Alan M. Voorhees Transportation Center.

Defines "advanced autonomous vehicle" as one with a driving automation level of 3, 4, or 5, per SAE J3016 standards.

Expires the act upon the report's submission.

DOC_ID: 474
Amends existing law to make it unlawful to knowingly purchase or possess visual depictions of a minor engaging in a prohibited sexual act or its simulation.

Requires separate prosecution and punishment for each different minor depicted in such visual content, though multiple depictions of the same minor are treated as a single offense.

Classifies a first offense as a class "D" felony and a second or subsequent offense as a class "C" felony.

Establishes that prior convictions or deferred judgments under this law, or similar laws in other jurisdictions, constitute second or subsequent offenses.

DOC_ID: 475
Amends Article 7, Chapter 57, Title 40 of the S.C. Code by adding Section 40-57-820.

Holds a licensee accountable for all work produced with the assistance of artificial intelligence, machine learning, or similar programs.

Treats violations committed through the use of these technologies as if committed directly by the licensee.
