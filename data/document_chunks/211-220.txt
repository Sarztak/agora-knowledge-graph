DOC_ID: 210
Defines "artificial intelligence" as a machine-based system generating outputs to influence environments, recognizing variations in autonomy and adaptiveness post-deployment. 

Mandates businesses to conduct risk assessments for using AI and automated decision-making technology when handling consumer data, especially for decisions impacting financial, employment, or educational opportunities.

Requires transparency through Pre-use Notices for consumers, detailing the use of AI in decision-making and providing opt-out or appeal options.

Specifies exceptions where consumers cannot opt-out, relying on the necessity of the use, with provisions for human review.

Demands businesses to ensure AI does not discriminate against protected classes through evaluations and safeguards.

Obliges businesses to disclose AI processes upon consumer request and prohibits retaliatory actions against consumers exercising their rights.

Mandates comprehensive documentation and regular updates for risk assessments, maintaining consumer privacy as a priority.

DOC_ID: 211
Implements Executive Order 14105 by requiring U.S. persons to notify the Department of the Treasury about transactions involving national security technologies in "countries of concern," and prohibits certain transactions that pose acute national security threats.

Defines AI systems as machine-based systems capable of making predictions, recommendations, or decisions that affect environments, thereby impacting transactions involving AI development and deployment.

Specifies which transactions are notifiable or prohibited, including those involving AI systems intended for military, intelligence, or surveillance uses as well as those of a specified computing power.

Mandates U.S. persons to file notifications and prohibits knowingly directing others to undertake prohibited transactions.

Details civil and criminal penalties for violations, including fines up to $250,000 or imprisonment up to 20 years.

Outlines exceptions, including certain public securities transactions and specific national interest exemptions.

Permits the Secretary of the Treasury to compel divestment of any prohibited transaction, with the aid of the Attorney General.

DOC_ID: 212
Ensure the safe, ethical, and fair use of AI technologies to protect personal data and privacy rights.

Apply to all stakeholders, including providers, users, importers, and distributors of AI systems.

Mandate adherence to principles of safety, transparency, fairness, accountability, and privacy in AI systems.

Require risk assessments and registration for high-risk AI systems with supervisory authorities.

Obligate AI operators to comply with the Law and allow supervisory authorities to monitor compliance and detect violations.

Impose fines up to 35 million Turkish Liras or 7% of annual turnover for violations, with varying penalties based on the nature of the breach.

Enforce the Law upon publication, with the President executing its provisions.

DOC_ID: 213

Mandates developers to establish anonymous internal reporting processes and ensures responsive investigation.

DOC_ID: 214

Mandates reviews of AI systems and algorithms adhere to national data security and ethical norms, ensuring transparency and reliability.

Introduces simplified and emergency review procedures for low-risk or urgent situations, with detailed conditions.

Calls for procedures to handle violations within AI ethics reviews, including potential penalties.

DOC_ID: 218
Establishes a program within the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) to improve precipitation forecasts.

Requires the use of fully coupled Earth System Models to enhance precipitation prediction.

Mandates the utilization of high performance computing and other technologies, including machine learning and artificial intelligence, to advance precipitation forecasts.

Supports research and development in collaboration with academic and private sector partners to test and evaluate emerging technologies.

Improves the accuracy, reliability, and timeliness of precipitation predictions across various timescales.

Incorporates social and behavioral sciences to enhance precipitation forecast products and communication.

Coordinates efforts with Federal, State, local, Tribal, and academic entities.

Authorizes appropriations of $15 million annually from fiscal year 2024 through fiscal year 2028 for the program.

DOC_ID: 219
Establishes the AI Grand Challenges Program to stimulate AI research, development, and commercialization through prize competitions addressing grand challenges in various sectors like national security, health, and environment.

Requires the Director of the National Science Foundation, in coordination with public and private entities, to identify AI grand challenges and develop problem statements.

Mandates the creation of a specific grand challenge for AI-enabled cancer breakthroughs.

Sets eligibility criteria for participants, requiring private entities to be U.S.-based and individuals to be U.S. citizens or permanent residents.

Requires submission of a report within 60 days of awarding a prize, detailing the winning submission and its benefits, as well as biennial reports to Congress on program activities and public outreach, to be made publicly accessible.
