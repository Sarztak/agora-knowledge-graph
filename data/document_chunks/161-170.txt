DOC_ID: 167
Requires healthcare entities in California using AI to comply with existing state laws related to consumer protection, civil rights, competition, and data privacy.

Prohibits the use of AI to deny health insurance claims or make medical decisions that override licensed care providers’ determinations.

Prohibits the use of AI systems that discriminate against patients based on historical access to healthcare.

Demands transparency and auditing of AI systems, ensuring they do not introduce bias or replicate human error.

Insists that entities uphold patient privacy, especially regarding sensitive information like reproductive and genetic data.

Mandates informed consent from patients when AI tools impact their healthcare.

Outlines existing protections under laws like California’s Unfair Competition Law, Unruh Civil Rights Act, and Confidentiality of Medical Information Act.

Instructs healthcare AI developers to prevent discriminatory impacts and consider the effects of training data.

Emphasizes the need for compliance with both state and federal regulations with no exceptions for AI-related practices.

Prohibits the involvement of AI as a defense to liability under any law.

DOC_ID: 168
Enacts the New York Artificial Intelligence Consumer Protection Act to manage risks of  AI decision systems. 

Requires developers to ensure systems do not cause algorithmic discrimination through regular audits, starting January 2027. 

Mandates developers provide comprehensive technical documentation and risk assessments to deployers. 

Obligates deployers to notify consumers when high-risk AI systems make significant decisions affecting them and to offer explanation and recourse for adverse decisions. 

Imposes a risk management program for deployers to identify and mitigate potential discrimination. 

Allows the attorney general to enforce compliance through audits and penalties, with the possibility of corrective actions to avoid litigation. 

Establishes exceptions for preempting federal law compliance and protects trade secrets from disclosure.
