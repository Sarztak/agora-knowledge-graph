DOC_ID: 61
Mandates the Secretary, with the Air National Guard Director, to study the necessity of acquiring advanced capability fighter aircraft for the Air Force.

Requires consideration of plans to implement unmanned or autonomous capabilities in fighter aircraft.

Includes evaluation of cost savings linked to foreign sales of F-16 variants.

Identifies eligible Air Force units for recapitalization with advanced fighter aircraft.

Determines whether a multiyear contract is the optimal acquisition method.

Obligates the Secretary to report findings to congressional defense committees within 180 days of enactment.

DOC_ID: 66
Regulates AI systems in Texas under the Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act.

Prohibits AI systems from inciting harm, criminality, manipulation, or discrimination based on political viewpoints or protected classes.

Establishes the Artificial Intelligence Council and a regulatory sandbox program for controlled AI testing.

Requires government disclosure to consumers when AI is used in interactions.

Instructs the attorney general to enforce regulations, manage complaints, and impose fines for non-compliance.

Prohibits government use of AI for social scoring or capturing biometric identifiers in violation of rights.

Mandates that AI must not produce certain unlawful content and must adhere to consumer protections and data privacy.

DOC_ID: 67
Mandates the development and implementation of policies to manage and utilize digital data for maintenance of aircraft, ships, and ground vehicles within one year.

Requires investment in advanced data infrastructure for efficient data processing.

Instructs the development of a strategy incorporating AI and automated systems for data organization.

Demands collaboration with vendors to overcome proprietary data limitations through open data approaches.

Addresses enhancement of data transmission capabilities with high-speed technologies and secure methods.

Mandates central compilation of maintenance data and creation of user interfaces for data analysis.

Requires vendor-agnostic, interoperable tagging systems unless exceptions are justified.

Calls for review of classification policies to ensure appropriate classification without limiting usability.

Establishes protocols for detecting unauthorized access to vehicle or platform systems.

Obligates a briefing to Senate and House Armed Services Committees on policy development and implementation status.

DOC_ID: 68
Requires the Secretary of Defense to initiate a pilot program within 60 days to evaluate AI-enabled software for optimizing workflows and operations at Department of Defense facilities, including depots and shipyards, and in contract administration.

Mandates the use of top-tier software platforms, consideration of industry best practices, implementation based on human-centered design, and connection to authoritative enterprise data sources.

Obliges consultation with the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition and Sustainment, and the Secretaries of the Army, Navy, and Air Force.
