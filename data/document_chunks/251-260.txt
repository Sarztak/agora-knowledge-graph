Establishes the AI Authority to ensure regulators take accountability for AI, align regulatory approaches, review legislation, monitor regulatory frameworks, assess risks, support testbeds, and promote international interoperability.

DOC_ID: 251
Prohibits the distribution of political communications containing materially deceptive AI-generated audio that impersonates a candidate’s voice, with actual malice, intended to harm the candidate’s reputation or deceive voters.

Applies to communications distributed within 90 days of a general election or 60 days of a primary, special, or runoff election.

Exempts certain entities from liability such as radio or television stations that air deceptive AI-generated content as part of news coverage or communications that clearly constitute satire.

Defines "materially deceptive audio" as AI-generated impersonation of a candidate’s voice without approval and lacking a clear disclaimer.

Imposes criminal penalties of fines or up to 2 years imprisonment for individuals who knowingly and willfully violate the prohibition.

Requires the Federal Election Commission (FEC) to issue regulations to clarify disclaimer requirements and mandates the submission of annual compliance reports to Congress.

Requires the Department of Homeland Security to develop a comprehensive plan within 180 days to identify, deploy, and integrate emerging and advanced technologies to enhance situational awareness along the northern and southern borders of the United States.

Mandates the inclusion of artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation, fiber-optic sensing, nanotechnology, optical and cognitive radar, modeling and simulation technology, hyperspectral and LIDAR sensors, and other similar technologies.

Includes an assessment of current technology utilization by U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) and how capability gaps can be addressed through technology integration.

Requires cost estimates for technology acquisition and maintenance, metrics for measuring technology impact, and an assessment of multi-domain awareness improvements.

Mandates an analysis of other Federal technologies, procurement authorities, and the transition of new technology systems.

Requires coordination with the Department of Homeland Security's Science and Technology Directorate for research, development, and incentivizing private sector innovation.

DOC_ID: 253
Prohibits U.S. persons from engaging in investments involving covered national security transactions in prohibited technologies, with exemptions permissible by the Secretary for activities deemed in the national interest. 

Allows the Secretary to issue regulations that require notifications for such transactions. 

Introduces penalties for violations, including civil penalties and potential divestment requirements, with the burden of proof resting on the Secretary in enforcement actions. 

Establishes processes for self-disclosure of violations and requests for non-binding feedback regarding transactions. 

Mandates annual reports to Congress detailing enforcement actions and technology assessments. 

Provides for confidentiality of transaction information, but outlines exceptions for disclosure relevant to U.S. national security interests. 

Encourages international cooperation to prevent prohibited technology development or acquisition by countries of concern, such as China. 

Defines key terms, including "prohibited technology" and "country of concern."

DOC_ID: 254
Expands the responsibilities of the Assistant Secretary of Commerce to include promoting the resilience of supply chains related to critical and emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence.

Mandates the creation of the Supply Chain Resilience Working Group composed of relevant federal agencies to assess and model supply chain stability, including those involving AI technologies.

Requires the group to identify vulnerabilities and prepare for potential supply chain shocks that might impact AI and other critical technologies.

Encourages domestic production and manufacturing, specifically focusing on AI and technological advancements.

Obligates periodic reports and assessments to Congress regarding the resilience and capacity of AI-related supply chains and industry preparedness for shocks.

Prohibits reliance on critical goods from countries identified as risks to national security, advocating for sourcing from allies.

Protects confidential data shared voluntarily under this title from disclosure, ensuring privacy concerns in relation to AI-related supply chain information.

DOC_ID: 259
Establishes protocols within the Frontier Safety Framework to address severe risks from future foundation models' capabilities.

Defines "Critical Capability Levels" (CCLs) to identify when AI models pose heightened risks, focusing initially on Autonomy, Biosecurity, Cybersecurity, and Machine Learning R&D.

Implements periodic evaluations to detect when models approach CCLs and articulates developing mitigation plans.

Proposes security mitigations to prevent model weight exfiltration and deployment mitigations to control access and expression of critical capabilities.

Outlines graded mitigation and security protocols based on AI capabilities and risks from potential misuse. 

Plans to implement the framework by early 2025 and expects it to evolve with emerging risks and insights.

Commits to periodically reviewing and updating the framework as understanding of AI risks and benefits improves.
