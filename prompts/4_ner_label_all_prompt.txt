ANNOTATION RULES

Exact phrase matching only — No partial or fuzzy matches.


No guessing — Label only if the text clearly matches a known entity or fits the description.

Mutual exclusivity — Each phrase can have only one label.


Non-overlapping spans — Do not assign two labels to overlapping text.


Precision over recall — Avoid false positives.
